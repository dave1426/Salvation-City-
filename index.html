"In that day shall this song be sung in the land of Judah; We have a strong city; salvation will God appoint for walls and bulwarks."
Isaiah 26:1 KJV
Our Mandate

Our Core Pillars

The foundational truth driving everything we do as a family of faith.

Redemption

Finding freedom and new life through the finished work of Jesus Christ. Your past does not define your future.

Restoration

Healing broken pieces, rebuilding families, and restoring peace and joy back into your everyday life.

Repositioning

Stepping into divine purpose, alignment, and authority to fulfill the extraordinary destiny God designed for you.

Join Us Live

Service Times & Location

We gather weekly inside our strong city to encounter God's presence in worship, receive practical biblical truth, and connect with an authentic community.

Sunday Celebration Service

10:00 AM – 11:30 AM (Every Sunday)

Daily Prayer & Fellowship

9:00 PM – 10:00 PM (Daily, except Saturdays)

Location

Open To Everyone
